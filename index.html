Settings & how to read a hand

Blue, red and black groups are three different suits. A hand written all in black is played in one suit. F = Flower · D = Dragon · N E W S = Winds · 0 = Soap (White Dragon), usable with any suit. Hands without exposures earn +10, except hands marked C.
